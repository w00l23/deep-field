About

Deep Field is one self-taught builder's set of quiet simulations of the space around us, made at night in plain HTML, with Claude as pair programmer. The maps, orbits and star positions come from NASA, JPL, USGS, ESA's Gaia mission and the Yale Bright Star Catalogue; each sim's folder credits them in full.
