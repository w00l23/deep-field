# Credits

`index.html` carries other people's measurements and pictures inside it,
copied as they are from the solar-system page. All of the pictures are public
domain (work of NASA). The Gaia mission and SIMBAD ask to be named; the rest
are named anyway.

| Inside the page | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `map-earth`, `map-land` | Earth's ground and shoreline | NASA Earth Observatory — Blue Marble: Next Generation, July 2004 (R. Stöckli) |
| `map-clouds` | Earth's clouds | NASA Earth Observatory — Blue Marble cloud composite |
| `map-night` | the lights of the cities | NASA Earth Observatory / NOAA — Black Marble 2016 (Suomi NPP) |
| `map-milkyway` | the Milky Way | NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio — Deep Star Maps 2020. Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC |
| `stars` | every star to magnitude 5.6 | Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th ed. (Hoffleit & Warren), via CDS Strasbourg, catalogue V/50 |
| `stars`, the distances | how far each of them is | the SIMBAD database, CDS, Strasbourg Astronomical Observatory, France |

None of the maps is the original file: each was reduced and re-encoded (WebP;
the shoreline is a PNG). Earth's land colour is carried a little past every
shore, and the Milky Way has the faint even glow of the whole sky taken off
and its bright end squeezed.

Saturn, its rings and its moons wear no map: they are painted by the page's
own shaders.

The sounds are made in the page, from oscillators and generated noise;
nothing in it is recorded.

The page loads [three.js](https://threejs.org) r170 (MIT licence) from the
jsDelivr CDN.
